- Domain: tool
- What went wrong or got revised: The `Sweaty Equity EP1` cameras and four speaker-mic rolls did not share trustworthy clock dates, and each mic restarted into later files. One timestamp-derived or global offset would have been indefensible.

### Reconcile one-frame still-image duration errors after append
- Domain: tool
