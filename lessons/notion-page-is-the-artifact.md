# Notion pages are the index and the artifact

source: mason. He corrected this on the Banking for Agents plan (2026-11-23) and again the next day on the Hero MCP brief, the ranked-list hub, and the holiday series page. Intent only.

The parent page is a short index. Child pages sit under it. Do not rebuild that as a link table, a priority column, or a numbered title list.

A child page holds the thing he asked for: the table, the graphic, the script, the brief. It does not explain why the work exists, what Slash is, the workflow, the open questions, or the next steps.

Writing stays plain. No "it's not X, it's Y." No em dashes. No stacks of fragment sentences. A brief is written to the person who will make the thing, without a process appendix.

Check: the parent is only the index, and a child page does not explain the job back to him.
