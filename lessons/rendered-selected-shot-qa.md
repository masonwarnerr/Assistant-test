# Inspect every selected shot in the rendered edit

Status: verified local worker procedure, 2026-10-18.

- Scope: camera-selection QA for edited interviews, demos, and multicamera cuts.
- Prerequisites: a rendered review movie and a manifest or timeline inventory of selected shot ranges.
- Source: same-machine source inspection, rendered correction checks, and complete review-render QA.

Sparse contact-sheet samples from each long source clip do not prove that every chosen timeline range is usable. Short repositioning, shake, obstruction, or camera-roll windows can fall between source samples and still land inside a selected shot.

Inspect the rendered midpoint of every selected shot. Flag short runs and any range whose framing, level, obstruction, or camera state contradicts the intended selection. A camera label such as “wide” or “close” is not quality evidence.

For a replacement, inspect early, middle, and late frames of the synchronized alternate before changing the edit. Preserve the prior revision, apply the replacement only to the new revision, and inspect the corrected rendered range again.

The acceptance check is complete selected-shot coverage in the rendered movie, a full frame decode with no unintended black frames, and explicit reinspection of every corrected range. If the full selected-shot inventory is unavailable, state the sampling limit instead of claiming complete visual QA.
