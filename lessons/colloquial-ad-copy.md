# Colloquial ad and caption copy

source: mason, stated 2026-11-12 as a correction he has to repeat. Intent only. No model or tool steps.

Ad, caption, and organic social copy should sound like one person talking to another. Professional vocabulary is fine. Story and a plain suggestion are fine.

Do not use "it's not X, it's Y." Do not stack short punch fragments. Do not write "if this, then that" as a cadence. Do not end on a fake quotable closer.

Angles, numbers, and disclosures stay. The voice is the part that was wrong.

Check: a read-through has none of those tells, and he does not have to ask for a second voice pass.
